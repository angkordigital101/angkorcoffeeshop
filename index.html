The Angkor Roasting Ritual

We respect the sacred integrity of every bean through precision time-temperature profiling on our vintage cast iron drums.

01

Highland Terroir

Hand-cultivated in the mineral-dense basalt volcanic soils of Mondulkiri and Ratanakiri at elevations up to 1,200 meters above sea level.

02

Manual Micro-Sorting

Only ripe, hand-picked cherries are selected. Before roasting, our artisans meticulously hand-inspect each batch, rejecting any defective bean.

03

Cast-Iron Drum Roasting

Roasted in intimate 12kg small batches on customized cast-iron drums to develop delicate sweetness and complex sugars without scorching.

04

Sensory Cupping & Packing

Every single roast batch is cupped for balance, aroma, and clarity, then sealed immediately in foil bags with one-way degassing freshness valves.

Artisan Brew Guides

Fine-tuned extraction recipes designed by our lead baristas to highlight origin nuances.

V60 Pour Over

Ideal for highlighting the delicate honey and floral notes of Mondulkiri highland peaberries.

Coffee / Water18g / 300g (1:16.6)
Grind SizeMedium-Fine (Sea Salt)
Water Temp92°C - 94°C
Total Brew Time2:45 - 3:00 min
Roaster Tip: Bloom with 50g of water for 45s to let carbon dioxide escape freely.

Traditional Phin Filter

The iconic Southeast Asian slow-drip method. Delivers dense, velvety chocolate body with condensed milk.

Coffee / Water22g / 120g (1:5.5)
Grind SizeMedium-Coarse
Water Temp95°C
Drip Rate40-50 drops per min
Roaster Tip: Pour over 1.5 tablespoons of sweet condensed milk and stir over ice.

Espresso Machine

Extracting the thick golden crema and dense hazelnut body of our Bayon Crema roast.

Dose / Yield19g In / 40g Out (1:2.1)
Grind SizeExtra Fine (Powder)
Pressure9 Bar Pressure
Extraction Time27 - 30 seconds
Roaster Tip: Ensure a flat, even tamp to prevent channeling and bitter over-extraction.

18h Nitro Cold Brew

Smooth, completely devoid of bitterness, highlighting chocolate fudge and vanilla sweetness.

Coffee / Water100g / 800g (1:8)
Grind SizeCoarse (Kosher Salt)
Steeping TempCold Filtered Water
Steep Duration18 - 20 Hours
Roaster Tip: Strain twice through unbleached paper filters for crystal clarity.

Born Under Ancient Skies

Honoring Sacred Soil & Highlands

Cambodia's coffee history is one of the world's most captivating, best-kept secrets. In the remote eastern highlands of Mondulkiri, mineral-rich red volcanic basalt soil and misty mountain micro-climates create conditions comparable to the finest coffee regions of East Africa and Central America.

Founded in Siem Reap, Angkor Coffee Roasters was established with a singular mission: to resurrect and champion Cambodian highland specialty coffee on the world stage. We collaborate directly with indigenous Bunong tribal families, providing organic farming support, harvesting training, and paying 45% above commodity market prices.

Frequently Asked

Roastery Questions & Answers

How fresh is my coffee when it arrives?
Every single order is roasted to order on our cast iron drums within 48 hours of dispatch. We stamp the exact roast date and batch code on the valve seal so you know precisely when it left the cooling tray.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes! We ship worldwide via DHL Express. Orders to Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, Australia, Europe, and the USA typically arrive within 3 to 5 business days, sealed with one-way degassing valves to preserve peak aroma.
Should I choose Whole Bean or Pre-Ground?
If you own a burr grinder, we always recommend Whole Bean for maximum aromatic longevity. However, if you prefer convenience, select the grind profile matched to your equipment (Espresso, Pour Over, Traditional Phin, or French Press).
What makes Mondulkiri Peaberry so rare?
Normally, a coffee cherry develops two flat beans facing each other. In approximately 5% of cherries on high-elevation Mondulkiri trees, only a single rounded bean develops—the Peaberry. This concentrates all sugars and nutrients into a dense sphere that roasts with exceptional evenness.

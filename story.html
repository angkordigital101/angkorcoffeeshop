Our Heritage & Philosophy

Sacred Soil, Highland Spirit

How a passion for Cambodia's ancient terroir ignited a specialty coffee renaissance in Siem Reap and the Mondulkiri highlands.

Chapter 01

The Forgotten Volcanic Highlands

Tucked away in the remote northeastern corner of Cambodia lies the Mondulkiri and Ratanakiri plateau. Blessed with deep crimson basalt volcanic soil, perpetual mountain mists, and elevations exceeding 1,100 meters, this land holds a natural affinity for cultivating world-class Arabica and unique micro-lot Peaberries.

For decades, these rare harvests remained hidden from global specialty coffee lovers. In 2021, Angkor Coffee Roasters embarked on a quest to revive and celebrate this extraordinary terroir.

Chapter 02

In Partnership With The Bunong

True specialty coffee begins with human dignity. We work in direct partnership with indigenous Bunong farming families who have stewarded Mondulkiri's sacred forest canopies for centuries.

We reject the destructive volatility of commodity brokers. Angkor Coffee pays our farming partners an average of 45% above Fair Trade minimums, while financing organic compost initiatives and clean water wells in farm villages.

Chapter 03

Cast-Iron Drum Alchemy

At our Siem Reap roastery lab located near the ancient temple city, coffee roasting is approached with mindful reverence. We use 12kg restored cast-iron drum roasters that provide radiant, conductible heat with unparalleled gentleness.

Every bean possesses a distinct voice. By tailoring the rate-of-rise (RoR) throughout the first crack and maillard phase, we preserve delicate jasmine and wild honey aromatics while coaxing out the velvety, dark chocolate body Cambodia is famous for.
